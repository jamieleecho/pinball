{ }Lost World Pinball
{ }for the CoCo 3

A remake of the 1983 Tandy MC-10 game, built on the DynoSprite engine.

{-}Controls

CONTROL - left flipper
SHIFT - right flipper
ENTER - hold to draw the launcher back, release to fire
BREAK - abandon the game
SPACE - start a new game

The arrow keys work as flippers too.

{-}Scoring

Bumpers - 10
Red power strips - 10
Plungers - 20
Red marks - 30

Hit every plunger during one ball and they all reset, with later scores doubled. Do it again for 3X.

Hit the red marks under the top pods twelve times and Vally catches the fly. The volcano erupts and the ball scores 10X.

An extra ball arrives with every 10,000 points.
